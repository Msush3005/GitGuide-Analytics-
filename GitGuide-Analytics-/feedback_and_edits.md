# Narrative Clarity Testing & Feedback Log

To test narrative clarity, we shared the draft narrative document with two non-technical readers (a marketing manager and an operations lead) and conducted the **3-Question Narrative Clarity Test**.

---

## 1. The Three Clarity Questions Asked

1. **Question 1**: What is the main finding of this analysis?
2. **Question 2**: What should the company do about it?
3. **Question 3**: Did anything confuse you or require second reading?

---

## 2. Peer Feedback Summary

### Tester 1: Marketing Operations Lead
- **Q1 Answer**: *"Customer churn is directly caused by slow support response times. Waiting over 24 hours makes customers 4x more likely to leave."*
- **Q2 Answer**: *"Hire 2 more support specialists, set a 2-hour SLA target, and give high-paying customers priority routing."*
- **Q3 Feedback**: *"The recommendation section was very clear, but in the original draft paragraph 3 you used the term 'support SLA latency' which sounded like tech jargon. Also, it wasn't clear how much money hiring 2 specialists would save."*

### Tester 2: Finance Business Partner
- **Q1 Answer**: *"Support speed drives churn. 2-hour responses keep churn at 3%, while 24-hour delays drive churn to 12%."*
- **Q2 Answer**: *"Add support headcount to reduce response times below 2 hours and implement priority queues."*
- **Q3 Feedback**: *"In Recommendation 1, state the net financial return directly in the bullet point so executives don't have to calculate the ROI in their heads."*

---

## 3. Revisions Made Based on Feedback

1. **Jargon Elimination**: Replaced the phrase *"support SLA latency"* with *"support response delay"* in Section 3.
2. **Quantified Financial ROI**: Added explicit net financial ROI to Recommendation 1 (*"recovering $400,000 in annual recurring revenue — a 2x net return on hiring costs"*).
3. **Bold Metric Highlights**: Bolded key financial figures (`$2,000,000`, `3.0%`, `12.0%`, `4x increase`, `$400,000`) throughout the text so executive readers scanning the document absorb core facts instantly.
