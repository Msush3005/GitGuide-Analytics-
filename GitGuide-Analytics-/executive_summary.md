# CHURN REDUCTION INITIATIVE
## Executive Summary

### Situation
Customer churn is our leading revenue loss driver, costing $2M annually. Our current annual churn rate stands at 7%, significantly exceeding the SaaS industry benchmark of 4%. Following a comprehensive operational investigation across 50,000 accounts, we identified a primary bottleneck: support response latency directly drives customer cancellation. Improving support response speed represents our highest-ROI revenue recovery opportunity.

### Key Findings
- **Support Speed Dictates Churn**: Customers receiving support within 2 hours churn at just 3%. Customers waiting >24 hours churn at 12%—a 4x increase.
- **Operational Bottleneck**: Our support team currently averages a 6-hour response time, missing the critical 2-hour retention window.
- **High-Value Exposure**: Accounts spending >$10K/year churn at 15% when support is slow, exposing our highest-margin revenue.

### Business Risks
- **$2M Annual Revenue Loss**: Unchecked 7% churn leaks $2M yearly, compounding ARR decay.
- **High-Value Churn Vulnerability**: Losing a single $500K account offsets a full year of support hiring savings.
- **Competitive Disadvantage**: Competitors with faster support SLAs are actively poaching our dissatisfied customers.
- **Operational Burnout**: Ticket volume is up 40% YoY while response times degrade, driving agent turnover and service quality collapse.

### Recommendations
1. **Hire 2 Support Engineers** (Cost: $200K/year)
   - Reduces average response time from 6 hours to <2 hours
   - Expected Impact: Recovers $400K in churned revenue annually (2x ROI in Year 1)
2. **Implement Response Time SLA** (Cost: $0)
   - Enforce <2 hour target with daily automated performance dashboards
   - Creates team accountability; implementation live by Jan 1
3. **Prioritize High-Value Customers** (Cost: $50K engineering)
   - Route >$10K/year accounts to a dedicated priority queue
   - Expected Impact: Reduces high-value churn by 50%; live by Feb 1

### Decision Needed
Approve hiring budget ($200K) and engineering resources ($50K) by Dec 15 to secure $400K net revenue recovery in Year 1.
